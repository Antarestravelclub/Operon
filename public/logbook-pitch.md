# My Logbook: The Asset Pedigree of the Outdoors

## 1. The Opportunity
High-value assets—boats, motorcycles, and RVs—are deteriorating in value due to the "Spreadsheet Silo" problem. Owners have no verifiable maintenance history, leading to thousands of dollars in lost resale value. 

## 2. The Solution
My Logbook is not just a digital ledger; it is a **portable Asset Pedigree**.
- **Real-Time Data:** Live trip tracking and fuel/maintenance capture.
- **Compliance & Value:** A maintenance log that carries forward during asset resale.
- **Smart Infrastructure:** BYOK "Cloud" architecture (Supabase + Mapbox) that scales without platform-taxed hosting costs.

## 3. The Growth Engine (Affiliate/Viral Loops)
We turn our users into our salesforce. 
- **Automated Bounties:** Every user has a unique referral code/QR generator.
- **In-Platform Incentives:** $4.99 commission payout for every referred subscriber.
- **Compound Acquisition:** Sticker/QR placement at high-traffic hubs (marinas, trailheads) drives self-boarding traffic.

My Logbook is built as a **"System of Record"** for leisure assets.
